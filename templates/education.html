Why Quantum Security Matters

Understanding the risks of today and the security of tomorrow

Modern encryption protects your data today — but quantum computing threatens to break it tomorrow.

Simulate Risk

How Your Data is Protected Today

Web security relies on TLS, RSA, and ECC to protect communications, identity, and key exchange between clients and servers.

RSA

RSA secures key exchange and signatures using large-number math. It is widely deployed but vulnerable to future quantum attacks.

ECC

ECC provides strong security with smaller keys and high performance. Many modern systems depend on ECC for TLS and certificates.

TLS Handshake

The TLS handshake negotiates algorithms and session keys. Its strength depends directly on the cryptographic primitives selected.

The Quantum Threat

Shor’s Algorithm can solve the hard math behind RSA and ECC. When fault-tolerant quantum machines mature, classical public-key systems become breakable.

Shor’s Algorithm can rapidly factor large integers and solve discrete logs, undermining current public-key trust.

Post-Quantum Cryptography

The next generation of cryptography is designed to resist both classical and quantum attacks.

Kyber

Key encapsulation mechanism for secure key exchange in quantum-safe TLS.

Dilithium

Digital signature algorithm designed for high assurance and practical deployment.

Falcon

Compact and efficient signature scheme for constrained systems.

SPHINCS+

Hash-based signature scheme with conservative long-term security properties.
